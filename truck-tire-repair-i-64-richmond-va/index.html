I-64 through the Richmond area

I-64 links Richmond with Hampton Roads to the east and Charlottesville to the west, and it runs together with I-95 through downtown Richmond. Freight moving to and from Hampton Roads shares this road with regional deliveries and construction trucks, so tire trouble can show up at any hour.

Where we cover on I-64

We cover downtown Richmond, eastern Henrico and the airport area, the I-64 and I-295 interchanges, the western Henrico side toward Short Pump, and farther west toward Goochland.

  • Trailer tire blowouts in traffic backups
  • Steer tire damage from road debris
  • Slow leaks found on the morning route
  • Tires failing at a yard or customer dock near the interstate

How to get help fast

  • Get the truck as far from traffic as you safely can and turn on your hazards.
  • Call (804) 821-0538 and tell us the highway, direction and the nearest exit or mile marker.
  • Tell us the tire position (steer, drive or trailer) and the size on the sidewall if you can see it, for example 295/75R22.5.
  • Prefer text? Send your GPS pin on WhatsApp and we will find you.

We work only on commercial trucks, semi-trucks, 18-wheelers, trailers and heavy equipment. We do not service passenger cars.

Frequently asked questions

Can you come to a yard or customer site instead of the highway?

Yes. We service trucks at yards, job sites, docks and truck stops as well as on the roadside.

Do you replace tires and also patch and plug?

Both. If the casing can be saved we can repair it. If not, we replace it with the right commercial tire for the position.

How do I get a price?

Call for a quote. Pricing depends on the tire size and position and on what is needed on site.
