What we handle on I-95

I-95 is the main north-south freight route through Richmond, and it carries long-haul and regional trucks around the clock. When a tire fails you need someone who brings the right commercial tire, the tools and the know-how to change it where you stopped, without a tow and without losing the load.

  • Blowouts, shredded casings and lost treads
  • Flats and slow leaks found on the shoulder or at a truck stop
  • Steer, drive and trailer positions
  • 22.5 and 24.5 commercial tire sizes
  • Patch and plug when the casing can be saved

How to get help fast

  • Get the truck as far from traffic as you safely can and turn on your hazards.
  • Call (804) 821-0538 and tell us the highway, direction and the nearest exit or mile marker.
  • Tell us the tire position (steer, drive or trailer) and the size on the sidewall if you can see it, for example 295/75R22.5.
  • Prefer text? Send your GPS pin on WhatsApp and we will find you.

Where we respond along I-95

  • Our dispatch covers Richmond, where I-95 and I-64 share the same road through downtown, the I-295 bypass, Chester and Petersburg to the south, and Ashland and Hanover County to the north. Beyond that we cover a 50-80 mile radius around Richmond.
  • Safety first: keep your hazards on, stay away from the traffic side of the truck and out of the lane. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.

We work only on commercial trucks, semi-trucks, 18-wheelers, trailers and heavy equipment. We do not service passenger cars.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get to me on I-95?

Our average arrival time around Richmond is 30-45 minutes. Distance, traffic and time of day change that, and the dispatcher gives you an honest estimate when you call.

Can you change the tire on the highway shoulder?

Yes, when it can be done safely where you are. If the spot is too dangerous, the dispatcher will help you decide whether to move to the next exit.

What should I tell dispatch?

Direction of travel, the nearest exit or mile marker, the tire position and, if you can read it, the size on the sidewall.

Do you work on passenger cars or pickups?

No. LCH Trucking works only on commercial trucks, semi-trucks, 18-wheelers, trailers and heavy equipment.
