Why the I-85 and I-95 junction matters

Petersburg, about 25 miles south of Richmond, is where I-85 splits off from I-95 and heads southwest toward North Carolina. Trucks switching between the two interstates pass through here all day, which makes it a common place for flats and blowouts after a long run.

What we do here

Dispatch sends a mobile unit to your location around Petersburg and along I-85, depending on distance from Richmond within our 50-80 mile service radius.

  • Emergency tire replacement on tractors and trailers
  • Patch and plug repairs
  • Rim damage checks on site
  • Fleet tire inspections at your yard

How to get help fast

  • Get the truck as far from traffic as you safely can and turn on your hazards.
  • Call (804) 821-0538 and tell us the highway, direction and the nearest exit or mile marker.
  • Tell us the tire position (steer, drive or trailer) and the size on the sidewall if you can see it, for example 295/75R22.5.
  • Prefer text? Send your GPS pin on WhatsApp and we will find you.

We work only on commercial trucks, semi-trucks, 18-wheelers, trailers and heavy equipment. We do not service passenger cars.
