What to include in a fleet tire request

  • Fleet or company name and best contact number
  • Tire sizes and quantities required
  • Vehicle type: tractor, trailer, truck, bus or other commercial vehicle
  • Whether you need new tires, used tires or roadside assistance
  • Preferred pickup or service location and timing

Specific services, scheduling, delivery and mobile availability must be confirmed directly with LCH Trucking. This page does not guarantee inventory or a response time.
